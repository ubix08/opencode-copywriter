## Authority-Building Strategy

### Why This Works for Rachid Hakim

1. **First-mover on OpenClaw content** — Despite 350K+ GitHub stars, there's a massive content gap for practical OpenClaw guides. Most existing articles are news/reviews, not how-to tutorials.

2. **Dual perspective** — Rachid's ERP consulting background + AI builder experience means content can bridge "enterprise process thinking" with "practical AI implementation" — a combination most AI content creators lack.

3. **Product flywheel** — Every how-to tutorial demonstrates the value of ready-to-use templates and tools, which ties directly into Gumroad products. The Agent Blueprint, VPS Guide, and Gumroad Factory all get natural placement.

4. **Comparison content** — "vs" articles targeting "Claude Code vs OpenCode" and "OpenClaw vs Claude Code" capture search traffic for people evaluating tools, positioning Rachid as the authoritative evaluator.
