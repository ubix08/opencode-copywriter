## Most SEO audits are either too shallow or too expensive.

You either run a free tool that gives you 3 generic suggestions — or hire an agency for $2,000. There's nothing in between for people who want a thorough audit they can do themselves.

**SEO Audit Pro is a guided HTML toolkit that walks you through a professional-grade audit in 15 minutes.** No API keys, no subscriptions, no monthly fees.

### What you get:

**🔍 Technical SEO Scanner**
- Crawlability & indexation checklist
- Core Web Vitals assessment guide
- Mobile-friendliness & page speed evaluation
- Structured data / schema markup audit

**📝 On-Page Analyzer**
- Title tag & meta description optimizer
- Header structure (H1-H6) review
- Keyword targeting & content gap analysis
- Internal linking audit with recommendations

**🔗 Off-Page & Backlink Review**
- Backlink quality assessment framework
- Competitor backlink gap analysis
- Disavow vs reclaim decision guide

**📊 Audit Report Generator**
- Auto-scored audit (0-100) with priority rankings
- Action items sorted by effort vs impact
- Printable checklist for client or personal use

**🎁 Bonus Materials Included**
- Technical SEO Checklist (50-point checklist)
- Keyword Research Template (100 keywords)
