## See exactly where your SaaS revenue is going.

Most SaaS founders track MRR. Few know their real LTV, churn trajectory, or when they'll hit breakeven. You're flying blind.

**This is a revenue intelligence engine for your SaaS.** Drop in your metrics. Get projections, cohort analysis, and growth levers ranked by impact.

**📈 MRR Projection Engine**
- Current MRR + 3 growth scenarios (conservative, target, aggressive)
- Churn rate analysis with trend detection

- Monthly cohort retention curves

- LTV:CAC ratio with payback period

- "What if I double trial conversion?" → see annual impact
