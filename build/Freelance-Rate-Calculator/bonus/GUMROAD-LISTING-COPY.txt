## Never guess your rates again.

You've lost projects because you quoted too high — or left money on the table because you quoted too low. Either way, you're guessing.

**The Freelance Rate Calculator turns pricing from a guessing game into a formula.** Enter your target income, expenses, and work style. Get your hourly, project, and retainer rates instantly.

### What you get:

**📊 3 Rate Views**
- Hourly rate (billable hours vs target income)
- Project rate (scope + risk-adjusted pricing)
- Retainer rate (monthly recurring revenue model)

**⚡ Smart Adjustments**
- Experience level multiplier (Junior → Expert)
- Industry markup (tech, creative, consulting)
- Geographic cost-of-living adjustment

**📈 What-If Scenarios**
- See how changing hours or income targets affects your rates
- Compare full-time vs freelance equivalent earnings
- Find your break-even rate

**🎁 Bonus Materials Included**
- Rate Negotiation Script — 5 email templates
- 10 Pricing Strategies for Freelancers
