## Stop juggling 5 tools to plan your content.

You're drafting in Notion, scheduling in Buffer, tracking in a spreadsheet, and still missing deadlines. There's a simpler way.

**The Content Calendar Planner replaces 3 tools with one HTML file.** Plan your month, batch your topics, and track every piece of content across every platform — without a subscription.

### What you get:

**📅 Monthly Calendar View**
- Drag-and-drop topic scheduling
- Multi-platform column view (blog, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletter)
- Theme & campaign tagging

**📝 Batch Planning Mode**
- Cluster related content into production batches
- Track draft → review → publish → promote stages
- Repurpose tracker (one article → 5+ social posts)

**📊 Performance Dashboard**
- Publishing frequency by platform
- Content type breakdown (how-to, listicle, case study, opinion)
- Monthly publishing streak tracker

**🎁 Bonus Materials Included**
- 50 Content Topic Ideas for Tech Creators
- Content Repurposing Matrix — 1 article into 12 assets
