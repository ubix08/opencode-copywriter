HOW TO USE IN 60 SECONDS
─────────────────────────
1. Double-click  Content-Calendar-Planner.html
2. Set your month and year
3. Add your content pillars/themes (e.g., tutorials, case studies, opinions)
4. Drag topics onto calendar dates
5. Assign platforms (blog, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletter)
6. Track each piece through draft → review → publish → promote
7. Use Batch Planning tab to cluster related work

PRO TIP: Plan 1 month ahead in a single 30-minute session.
Batch all production work into 2-3 days per month. Your content
quality will improve and you'll stop the daily scramble for ideas.
